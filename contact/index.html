Please stay safe

Do not send private files or passwords

Do not email Minecraft account details, Microsoft or school passwords, private student information, or a world file containing information you do not want to share. SkyblueMC will never ask for your password.

For privacy requests, include “Privacy request” in the email subject and describe what you need. The website does not provide user accounts, so there is usually no account record to locate.
